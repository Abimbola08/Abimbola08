### Hi, I'm Abim 👋

I'm a Traffic/ITS Engineer at [Gresham Smith](https://www.greshamsmith.com/) in Atlanta and the founder of [SR4A — Safety and Resilience for All](https://sr4a.com). I hold a Ph.D. in Transportation Systems from UNC Charlotte, where my research built machine learning systems for weather-related crash prediction: geospatial analytics and ML for crash risk mapping, severity prediction, and forecasting.

**What I work on**

- 🚦 Traffic operations and forecasting for Georgia DOT programs — signal performance measures (ATSPM), microsimulation, traffic impact analysis
- 🌧️ Weather-related crash risk: spatiotemporal risk mapping, imbalanced-data severity models, forecast-conditioned deep learning
- 🚗 Public perception of automated vehicles and agency readiness for connected-vehicle data
- 🗺️ Building [SAFER Lab](https://sr4a-saferlab.github.io), SR4A's research lab — first project: an open, GIS-ready **road inventory for Nigeria**, starting at the local government level

**Toolkit:** Python · R · XGBoost · ConvLSTM · GIS · Power BI · VISSIM · TransModeler · SIDRA · ATSPM
